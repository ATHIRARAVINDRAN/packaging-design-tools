# Packaging production handoff checklist

Use this as a job worksheet. Fill in the named recipient and approved revision, then check the exported files rather than relying only on the open design. This is a practical checklist, not a manufacturing certification.

## Brief and structure

- [ ] Record the product measurements, clearance and intended opening direction.
- [ ] Name the structure, substrate and actual board caliper agreed with the converter.
- [ ] State whether dimensions mean inside space, outside footprint or score-to-score measurements.
- [ ] Identify every separate part and each printed side.
- [ ] Check the proposed closure, tabs, slots, glue areas and assembly sequence.

The [box size calculator](https://dielinemaker.com/tools/box-size-calculator) illustrates why a structure and its board must be considered together. Different wall constructions need different allowances; avoid assuming one thickness adjustment fits every box.

## Artwork and proof

- [ ] Check the front, back, side and inside artwork in the intended folded orientation.
- [ ] Keep critical copy within the agreed safe area and supply the specified bleed.
- [ ] Inspect text, images, barcodes and QR codes in the actual output.
- [ ] Name the client proof revision and record what was approved.
- [ ] Keep the proof distinct from the technical manufacturing deliverables.

## Files and receiving requirements

- [ ] Confirm units, scale, page size and the requested file format.
- [ ] Make technical cut and crease geometry distinguishable from printed content.
- [ ] Inspect every part, side and archive member; remove ambiguity about revision names.
- [ ] Confirm the agreed color workflow; choosing an output intent does not itself convert artwork color.
- [ ] Where relevant, agree sheet margins, grain direction, nesting and production quantity.

See [print checks and exports](https://dielinemaker.com/learn/print-checks-and-exports) and [sheet layout and nesting](https://dielinemaker.com/learn/sheets-and-nesting) for the corresponding DielineMaker workflow.

## Physical acceptance

- [ ] Print at actual size, cut, fold and assemble a sample.
- [ ] Check product fit, closing, reopening and the intended handling conditions.
- [ ] Obtain converter acceptance of the structure and production files before commissioning a die or production run.
- [ ] Archive the accepted files with the job brief and revision record.

**Does completing the list guarantee production success?** No. The receiving manufacturer still determines process suitability and any further testing.
